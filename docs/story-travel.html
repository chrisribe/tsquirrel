Venice's day-trip fee has ended for 2026

🐿️ The Squirrel's Take

That spring travel guide telling you to buy an entry code needs an update. Your hotel bill is a separate question.

Venice's official access-fee portal says the 2026 trial has ended. From July 27, visitors no longer need to pay the access fee or request an exemption. Any return of the charge after 2026 requires a further decision by the city.

🐿️ A little deeper

The charge at your hotel isn't the one that ended

Extra context · 2 min read

You arrive in Venice in September, having read that the visitor fee is gone. At checkout, your hotel still charges a tourist tax. Those two things can both be correct.

Venice has two different charges. The access fee covered day visitors on selected dates. The tourist tax is collected by accommodation providers and calculated per guest, per night. Ending the first does not cancel the second. [2]

They were not meant to stack up for the same stay. During the access-fee period, guests staying in accommodation within the Municipality of Venice were exempt from the entry charge, from arrival through departure. They still had to follow the exemption procedure. Now that the trial has ended, the city says no access-fee exemption request is needed either. [2] [1]

A week-long stay does not mean seven taxable nights

The city's FAQ gives the overnight tax a five-night cap. For a guest to whom the tax applies, a seven-night stay in the same accommodation is therefore not seven nights of tourist tax. The nightly rate is a separate question—ask the property which municipal rate and any exemptions apply to your booking. [2]

That also explains why the old €5 or €10 entry-fee figure is the wrong number to use when checking a hotel bill. Those were the access-fee prices, not a flat nightly hotel tax. [1]

For a September visit: you do not need to buy an access code. For an overnight stay, check the accommodation tax separately. And for a later year's trip, check the city's new rules rather than assuming this year's calendar repeats.
