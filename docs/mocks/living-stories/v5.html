Riverton council moves to replace transit fares with a flat monthly pass

Update · Nov 2: The council's finance committee approved the pass budget 7–2. A final council vote is set for Nov 18.

Riverton is weighing a switch from per-ride fares to a single $40 monthly pass for its bus and light-rail network. Supporters say it would simplify travel and raise ridership. Critics question how the city would cover the revenue gap.

Why now

The current fare contract ends in March. If the council doesn't decide by year-end, the city has to renew the old system for another three years.
