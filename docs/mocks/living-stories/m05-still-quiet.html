A Montreal startup's tiny rover is weeks from the Moon's south pole

Update · Nov 2: The rover passed its final vibration test, and the launch provider confirmed a Nov 18 launch window.

Borealis Robotics, a 40-person Montreal team, is sending a shoebox-sized rover to the Moon's south pole to look for water ice. If it lands and drives, it will be the first Canadian-built rover on the Moon.

Why now

The launch window opens Nov 18. Ice near the south pole could one day supply drinking water and rocket fuel, which is why the next crewed missions plan to land in the same region.
